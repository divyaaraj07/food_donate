## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the cultural ethos of *Anna Daan* (the sacred gift of food) transformed into modern civic infrastructure. The UI balances grassroots warmth and community generosity with the organizational rigor required for high-stakes, time-sensitive food rescue and redistribution across India.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
- **Warm Civic Modernism**: Contemporary digital precision anchored by organic Indian materiality. It avoids faux-heritage clutter by deploying clean layouts, generous negative space, and disciplined typographic hierarchy.
- **Micro-Heritage Accents**: Geometric rangoli and mandala patterns appear solely as delicate mathematical watermarks (opacity ≤ 8%), never as decorative noise. Icons reference indigenous forms—such as the earthen diya, wheat sheaves (dhanya), and brass tiffin tiers—rendered in minimalist, single-stroke vector lines.
- **Emotional Resonance**: Dignified, vibrant, prompt, and communal. The aesthetic evokes the warmth of a community kitchen (*langar*) paired with the operational clarity of emergency dispatch software.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from ceremonial spices, fertile earth, and sacred harvest colors, balanced against soft, unbleached grain backgrounds to reduce eye strain in outdoor field conditions.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#D97706` Terracotta Saffron)**: Signifies action, dispatch, primary calls-to-action, and community warmth. Used for hero buttons, active route highlights, and prominent milestones.
- **Secondary (`#15803D` Sacred Emerald)**: Signifies fresh surplus, safe consumption windows, verified hygiene credentials, and vegetarian indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` Marigold Amber)**: Reserved for urgent rescue calls, expiring pickups, and celebratory distribution tallies.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1C1917` Spiced Charcoal)**: High-contrast text and grounding structures. Warm undertones prevent clinical harshness.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFDF7` Warm Malai Cream)**: The foundational background, replacing sterile digital white with natural parchment warmth.

### Functional Dietary & Verification Semantics
- **Pure Veg Indicator**: Green dot inside a square frame (`#15803D`).
- **Non-Veg Indicator**: Deep carmine dot inside a square frame (`#B91C1C`).
- **FSSAI & Hygiene Verified**: Emerald badge with subtle golden-amber shield detail.
- **Emergency Pickups (< 2h shelf life)**: High-contrast terracotta-tinted banner with pulsing amber badge.

## Typography

The type system blends the energetic curves of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headers, wayfinding badges, and calls-to-action with the utilitarian legibility of **Inter** for dense transactional lists, logistical schedules, and food inspection logs.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Injected with warmth, gentle terminals, and crisp geometric structures that read cleanly across Indian regional script transliterations and bilingual headers.
- **Inter**: Neutral, horizontally compact, and legible on low-cost mobile displays under bright sunlight.
- **Numerals**: Numerical tallies (e.g., "1,240 kg Meals Rescued") always use tabular lining figures in Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold to prevent layout shifting during real-time dashboard updates.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured responsive grid built to support volunteer dispatchers on desktop consoles as effortlessly as volunteers reviewing pickup addresses on budget mobile browsers.

### Grid Rules
- **Desktop (≥1024px)**: 12-column fluid grid, 1280px max-width wrapper, 32px margins, 20px gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid, 24px margins, 16px gutters.
- **Mobile (≤767px)**: 4-column grid, 16px outer margins, 12px gutters.

### Layout Principles
- **Touch Ergonomics**: All interactive elements (accept donation, verify OTP, call venue) maintain a vertical tap target of at least 48px on mobile views.
- **Density Tiering**: Listing interfaces utilize tight, information-dense clusters (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) for food temperature and shelf-life metrics, surrounded by generous section separation (`space-xl`) to prevent accidental interactions in high-stress logistics environments.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, sunlit drop shadows combined with layered canvas surfaces rather than stark grey drop-shadows.

### Layer Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas)**: `#FFFDF7` base surface. Flat and grounded.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Shelves)**: `#FFFFFF` panels elevated by a warm ambient shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.05), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.04)`. Outlined with a soft cream-border: `1px solid rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Action Bars & Dropdowns)**: Elevated by `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Emergency Alerts)**: `0px 16px 36px -6px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a semi-opaque backdrop: `rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.6)` with a 3px blur.

### Decorative Vector Motifs
- Rangoli and geometric mandala motifs must be rendered as vector borders or background watermarks with absolute opacity limits between 4% and 7%. They are always positioned in layout corners or card margins and never layered directly beneath small body copy.

## Shapes

The interface balances organic hospitality with systematic card boundaries.

- **Baseline Geometry**: A 0.5rem (8px) base radius across operational cards, form inputs, and status displays. This softens the technological edge, reflecting the human kindness of food sharing without drifting into cartoonish aesthetics.
- **Pill Enhancements (`rounded-full`)**: Strictly reserved for contextual tags, dietary classification dots, filter pills, and quick-call action buttons.
- **Icon Enclosures**: Circular or soft squircle backdrops for rescue milestone icons, donor category indicators (e.g., Wedding Hall, Gurdwara Langar, Corporate Cafeteria), and vehicle dispatch trackers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Claim Food / Donate Meal)**: Solid `#D97706` terracotta saffron background, white `#FFFFFF` text, `border-radius: 8px`, inner letter-spacing `0.01em`. On hover: `#B45309`. On active: subtle scale shift down to 0.98.
- **Secondary (Volunteer / Route Info)**: Tinted background `rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.10)`, text `#15803D`, border: `1px solid rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.25)`.
- **Emergency Priority**: Terracotta-filled pulse button with high-contrast text and a miniature countdown timer.

### Badges & Category Tags
- **Dietary Indicators**: Standard Indian FSSAI compliant iconography.
  - *Pure Veg*: Square box (`#15803D`, 1.5px stroke) with a solid centered circle of 6px diameter.
  - *Non-Veg*: Square box (`#B91C1C`, 1.5px stroke) with a solid centered circle of 6px diameter.
- **Donor Origin Chips**:
  - *Langar Seva*: Warm amber container (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`), text `#B45309`, accompanied by a community pot icon.
  - *Wedding Caterer*: Terracotta container (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12)`), text `#D97706`.
  - *Halwai Surplus*: Warm honey-tinted container with dessert platter micro-icon.
- **Trust Badges**: "FSSAI Verified Kitchen" badge in rich green with a gold checkmark icon, uppercase `label-sm` tracking.

### Cards & Rescue Listings
- **Food Lot Card**: White card with a top 4px accent stripe color-coded by shelf life remaining (Green: >4h, Amber: 2-4h, Crimson: <2h).
  - Contains meal count badge (e.g., "Feeds 150 people").
  - Clear pickup window timestamp in tabular numerals.
  - Verification chip and venue distance indicator.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs**: White background, `1px solid rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.15)` border, 8px radius. Active focus transitions to `2px solid #D97706` with an offset ring of `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 4px radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Checked state fills with `#15803D` or `#D97706` with crisp white check/pip.
- **OTP Verification Inputs**: Segmented 6-box input array with elevated numeric styling, optimized for quick SMS confirmation at donation handover points.
